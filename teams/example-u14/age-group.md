# About the age group

> **Example content.** This squad is invented. It shows what an `age-group.md` covers and roughly how long it should be — replace it with your own.

Squad context for this age group: who the players are, when and where we train, and how we split them into training groups. Read this alongside `coaching.md` before producing session plans, playbooks, or drills.

**This doc is always the team's own.** There is no shared default for it, because none of it generalises.

## Squad

We run two teams:

- **Ash** — the more experienced side. Most have played since U9.
- **Elm** — developmental. Several players are in their first or second season.

**Squad physical & skill profile:** a wide spread of size within the age group, which matters most in contact. **Handling under pressure** and **tackle technique** are the two weakest foundations across both sides — they are what everything else is built on, so they recur every session rather than getting a block of their own and then being dropped.

**Where the group is now:** settled and willing, and able to take a new idea by being asked rather than told. Plan on asking.

**Neurodiversity:** several players in the squad are neurodiverse. Session design and delivery should take that into account — clear instructions, predictable structure, consistent naming, and a warning before anything changes. It is also why `playbook.md` keeps calls short, single-word and unchanging: a predictable call costs less to process under pressure than an ad-hoc instruction.

Record here anything a coach genuinely needs in order to plan well — and **nothing about an identifiable child.** These pages are public. No names, no medical detail, no "the tall lad who…". "Several players are neurodiverse, so structure matters" is useful and safe; anything narrower is neither.

## Training & Fixtures

- **Matches:** Sundays. Sometimes only one of the two sides has a fixture, in which case the other trains — check each separately.
- **Sunday training:** on the Sundays without a match. **90 minutes in total including the warm-up and the close** — plan to that, not to the length of the allocated slot, which absorbs arrival and a late start.
- **Midweek training:** Thursday evenings, on the floodlit training area.
- **Evenings draw in through the autumn.** Each evening plan carries the sunset time in its logistics, so a coach can see how much of the session is in daylight.

## Training groups

- Forwards and backs split for set-piece work, and rejoin for everything else.
- For games, the squad splits into **the session's two teams**, picked during the warm-up and kept all session (see `coaching.md`).

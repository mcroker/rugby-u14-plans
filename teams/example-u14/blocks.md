## Block 1 (Weeks 1–6)

### Overview & objectives

**Theme: the tackle, and the two-second ruck.**

Both are in the guiding principles (see `playbook.md`) and both are where this squad is weakest, so the block does one thing properly rather than three things thinly. Per the **skills pyramid** (see `coaching.md`), this block lives at the individual- and small-group-skill layers; team shape comes in Block 2.

- **Tackle:** the base position first, then the cheek-to-cheek contact, then getting up. Technique in the skill zone, applied in a game the same session — never one without the other.
- **Ruck:** two players, arriving low, ball available. The number we coach to is **two seconds**, and from Week 3 we time it in the game and say the number out loud.
- **Handling under pressure** runs through every session as the warm-up's skill content, because it is the other weak foundation and it does not get a block of its own.

# Calendar

> **Example content.** Invented dates and opposition, to show the shape.

**Keep this page non-PII.** It is public. Event, date, time, venue, which side, and the opposition club — and **nothing else**. No player, parent or guardian names, no contact details, no RSVP or attendance data, no medical information. If your club's calendar system exports more than that, take only these columns.

## Recurring training

- **Thursday evenings**, 18:45–20:15, on the floodlit training area.
- **Sundays**, 10:45–12:30, when there is no fixture.

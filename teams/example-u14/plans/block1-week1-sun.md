## Initial setup

Out before the players arrive, and nothing gets re-coned all session.

- **Game pitch** — 40m × 30m in **colour A**: a cone at each corner and one either side at halfway.
- **Thirds** — a line of three cones across the pitch at each third, in **colour B**. These stay down all session.
- **Tackle area** — mats down in the far third, two stations, 10m apart.
- **Ruck shields** — at the cone line ~20m out from the try-line end, for the warm-up.

## Activities

### Rainbow Passing

**Groups:** whole squad, in two lines facing each other.

**Setup:** Coloured cones in two parallel lines, matched colour opposite matched colour. 5m between the lines to start.

**Coaching Points:** Hands out early, elbow up, catch in front of the body. Eyes on the next receiver before the ball arrives, not after.

**Description:**

1. Players jog along their line; the coach calls a colour.
2. The player level with that colour passes across to the player opposite.
3. The receiver carries two steps and returns it.
4. Keep moving; the coach keeps calling.

**Progressions:**

- Add a second ball once the first is clean.
- Call two colours at once so two passes are live.

**Adaptations:**

- Passes too easy: widen the gap between the lines.
- Passes falling short: narrow it rather than dropping the tempo.
- Group too large: run two sets side by side.

### Tackle base position

**Groups:** four groups of five or six, two per mat station, rotating.

**Setup:** Two mat stations 10m apart. One coach on each. Tackler starts on their knees for the first round.

**Coaching Points:** **Feet, chest, eyes** — that is the whole cue set this week. Feet close before contact, chest up and square, eyes open and on the target. Nobody leads with the head.

**Description:**

1. From knees: tackler in position, ball-carrier walks in, tackler makes cheek-to-cheek contact and holds. No finish.
2. From a crouch: same, ball-carrier at walking pace.
3. Standing, ball-carrier at a jog, tackler completes and gets straight back to their feet.

Two or three each per round, then rotate the group.

**Progressions:**

- Ball-carrier picks a side late, so the tackler has to adjust their feet.
- Tackler gets up and back into position as a defender straight after.

**Adaptations:**

- Group nervous or mixed in size: pair by size, and stay at stage 1 longer.
- Ground hard: mats only, and drop the volume rather than the technique.

### Two-Second Touch

**Groups:** the session's two teams, whole pitch.

**Setup:** 40m × 30m, cones already down. Bibs on one side.

**Coaching Points:** Two players to the ruck, no more. Ball available on the floor within **two seconds** — the coach counts it out loud so the number means something. Support arrives before the touch, not after.

**Description:**

Touch rugby. On a touch the carrier goes to ground and presents the ball back; a support player picks and plays. The coach counts "one — two" aloud on each recycle. A team that beats the count three times in a row gets a free try's worth of points.

**Progressions:**

- Drop to a one-second count for one team only, to show the difference.
- Turnover if the recycle takes longer than three seconds.

**Adaptations:**

- Attack getting out too easily: narrow the channel to two-thirds width.
- Defence overrunning: require two defenders to stay behind the ball.
- Numbers uneven: the short side gets an extra touch before a turnover.

### Two-Second Touch — narrowed

**Groups:** the same two teams, on two-thirds of the pitch.

**Setup:** Move the game inside the thirds cones already down. No re-coning.

**Coaching Points:** Same three points as before. The space is the progression — everything else stays identical so the players notice what changed.

**Description:** As above, in the narrower channel. Last ten minutes, full width restored, count kept.

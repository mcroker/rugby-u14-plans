# Playbook & Calls

> **Example content.** These calls are invented, and deliberately few — a playbook that fits on a page is a playbook players remember. Copy `content/playbook.md` (the empty skeleton) rather than this file when you start your own, so you don't inherit somebody else's calls.

Our calls and shapes for the squad. **Players-shareable:** no squad or coaching context on this page — that lives in `coaching.md`.

## Playing philosophy

**Move the ball before contact, not after it.** We are not the biggest side we will meet, so a game of collisions is a game we lose. Quick hands, early decisions, and a ruck that is over in two seconds.

### Guiding principles

Every session should map back to at least one of these. A drill that maps to nothing is a drill we don't need.

- **Two-second ruck.** That is the number we coach to, and we time it.
- **Two players to the ruck, no more.** In fast, ball available, no turnover.
- **Eyes up before the ball arrives.** Decide early; the catch is not the moment to start looking.
- **Line speed off the line-out and the tackle.** Take the space before they use it.
- **Fitter than the opposition**, earned through how sessions are run, not through fitness work.

## Calls

Calls are **one word**, said loudly, and mean the same thing every time. Nobody has to think about what a call means under pressure — that is the entire point, and it matters most for the players who find processing an instruction hardest.

### Open play

| Call | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Wide** | Ball is slow, defence is narrow | 9 skips the first pod, ball straight to 10 and out. Nobody sets. |
| **Short** | Defence has drifted, space inside | 9 goes to the first forward off the base, one-out, straight up the middle. |
| **Hold** | Ball is quick, defence is retreating | Forwards stay off the ruck. Play the next phase before they reset. |

### Defence

| Call | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Up** | Our default from a set piece | The whole line comes off the line together, fast, from the inside out. Nobody drifts. |
| **Hold** | Close to our own line | Line speed drops, spacing tightens, nobody bites in. |

**Two pillars either side of every ruck**, before anyone looks up. That is not a call; it happens every time.

## Set piece

### Lineout

Three calls, and they are colours so nobody has to remember a number under pressure:

| Call | Where the ball goes |
|---|---|
| **Blue** | Front |
| **White** | Middle |
| **Green** | Back |

The hooker calls it, and calls it late. Catch, turn, ball to 9 — no maul off this at our age group unless we are inside their 22.

### Scrum

Straight ball, 8 controls at the base, 9 takes it when 8 says so and not before. Our own put-in is an attacking platform; theirs is a chance to set the defensive line early.

## Restarts

Chase in a line, not as individuals. The two tallest contest; everyone else is there to win the ball on the floor.

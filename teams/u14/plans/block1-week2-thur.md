## Activities

### Essential skills — passing (Crazy Passing)

**Groups:** two sets — three lines of four or five in each.

**Coaching Points:** elbow up, hands out, catch early. Call for it before it comes. With two balls, know where both are without looking.

**Setup:** three lines (A, B, C) spaced for passing, two sets side by side in the near third. Split the squad however you like — this one does not use the night's teams. Full detail in `activities.md`.

**Description:** the ball zig-zags down the lines, each player following their pass to the back of the line they passed to. Second ball in once the pattern is clean.

**Progressions:**
- Second ball in.
- Widen the spacing.
- Time a full circuit, then beat it.

**Adaptations:**
- Pattern breaking down: take the second ball out and rebuild.
- Passes dropping short: shorten the spacing.
- Poor form in two or three: a spare coach takes them off the side, in pairs, while the drill runs.

### Vocal warm-up — everyone shouts

**Groups:** whole squad, one circle.

**Coaching Points:** everyone at once, loudly. Two minutes, no more, then straight into the game.

**Setup:** one circle, no equipment, coach in the middle.

**Description:** three steps, two minutes.

1. **All together** — on three, everyone shouts something daft. Twice, second one louder.
2. **Teams against each other** — the two teams out-shout each other. Judge it badly and obviously.
3. **Round the circle** — own name in turn, fast, no gaps.

Then stop, and don't explain it.

**Adaptations:**
- **Offer the opt-out to the whole squad before you start** — clap or stamp instead of shouting.
- Step 3 dying: back to step 1 and finish there.
- Something different to shout from last week.

### Game 1 — touch, Bang and Springbok Touch

**Groups:** one game, the night's two teams whole — 13 v 14 or thereabouts. Not two small games.

**Coaching Points:** line-speed off **Bang**; spacing, numbering up, the shuffling retreat, talk. Two seconds at the ruck. Coach it in the stoppages the game gives you, one or two players at a time.

**Setup:** the full square. **Presentation Touch** — on a touch the carrier goes down, turns, and presents long (see `activities.md`). Every restart is a **tap** on the spot; no lineout and no scrum in either game. Eighteen minutes, straight through.

**Description:** Sunday's game again, on the smaller space. Two conditions, both already known:

1. **Bang** — the blitz trigger (see `playbook.md`). Third outing; expect one reset, not three.
2. **Springbok Touch** — first receiver caught still holding the ball is a turnover, taken as a tap to the defence (see `activities.md`).

**Progressions:**
- **Heard it** — the turnover only counts if **Bang** came from the line's nominated caller and the line went together. Add it once the game is flowing, and leave it on into Game 2.
- Rotate the nominated caller every few phases.
- *If the talk has gone flat:* two minutes of **silence** — no defensive talk — then take it off.

**Adaptations:**
- Attack getting out too easily: bring the touchlines in a third. Defence smothering everything: widen it back out.
- One team running away with it: swap a pair over.
- Numbers low: drop to one team of whoever is there against the rest, still one game.

### Contact warm-up (whole squad)

**Groups:** whole squad, in pairs matched by size.

**Coaching Points:** in the pummel — chest up, back flat, **head to the side**, hips low, arms working continuously. In Wrist Wrestle — stay low, feet moving, win it with body position.

**Setup:** everyone in pairs, **matched by size**. No equipment. Water first, then straight into it.

**Description:** the standard two minutes — demo not needed.

1. **Get low (~30s)** — on the spot, feet moving, drop into the tackle-ready position. Five or six reps.
2. **Pummelling (~45s)** — pairs chest to chest **on knees**, one arm inside and one outside, both swimming the outside arm inside on the call.
3. **Wrist Wrestle (~45s)** — standing, same-wrist grips locked, free hand behind the back, slap with the held hand. A point per clean touch, then reset.

Rules unchanged: pummelling is an arm battle — no lifting, no takedowns, no twisting a neck. Wrist Wrestle targets **shoulder or upper arm only**, and stops the moment a grip goes. **Anyone who would rather not play the slapping game pummels again instead — offer it to the whole squad up front.**

**Adaptations:**
- Anyone new or back from injury: start at stage 1, join the games when a coach says so.
- Badly mismatched pair: break the three and rotate.

### Scrum — body position, positional groups, the machine (forwards)

**Groups:** all forwards plus both 9s — ~14. Three positional groups, one coach each.

**Coaching Points:** **shoulders above hips, always.** Straight back, chin off the chest, eyes up, neck strong. Bind before anyone pushes. Nobody moves until **set**. A scrum going down means everybody stops — say it once, out loud, before the first engage. **One coach per group, and the safety call comes from that coach only.**

**Setup:** at the machine, not on the pitch — **walk them over at the start of the block and back at the end, and the walk is inside the twenty-four minutes.** Front rows picked for size, not for who wants to be there. **Non-contested all night: no pack against pack.** Five parts, in this order.

**Description:**

**1. Walk over (~2 min).**

**2. Body position (~5 min).** Sunday's work, sharpened, not re-taught.

1. Drop into the tower of power on a call, hold, get it checked, reset. Five or six.
2. **Bear crawl** — flat back, hips low, head up. Out and back, freezing into the position on a call.
3. In pairs, one holds the position while the other pushes on the shoulders — resist without standing up or dropping the head.

**3. Positional groups (~8 min).** Three at once, one voice each.

| Group | Who | The job |
|---|---|---|
| **a** | **Front row + both 9s** | The bind — hooker gripping both props, props square. Then **9–hooker comms and the put-in**: 9 checks the hooker is ready, one word for the timing, feed straight and fast to the strike foot. Hooker strikes **on the feed**. Both 9s rotate through. |
| **b** | **Back row — 6, 7, 8** | Where each binds: 6 and 7 onto the locks, heads in the gap; **8 between the locks**, gripping their shorts. Then **8 controlling the ball at the base with his feet** — keep it in, keep it there, and it does not leave until 9 asks. Stay bound until the ball is out. |
| **c** | **Second row — the locks** | Head into the gap between prop and hooker, **inside arm through the prop's legs onto the shorts**, outside arm onto the flanker. Feet staggered, weight forward, drive straight on **set**. Then two-on-a-shield drives, both locks square. |

**4. On the machine (~7 min).** The eight together, one pack at a time if two are out.

1. **Build the bind standing** — front row, locks in, back row. Walk five steps as one unit.
2. **Crouch–bind–set**, coach calling the cadence. Five or six.
3. **The shove** — everyone goes on one word, together.
4. **The full sequence** — cadence, shove, **9 feeds, hooker strikes, 8 controls, ball out to 9**. Six to eight, then stop.

**5. Walk back (~2 min).**

**Progressions:**
- 9 varying the timing so the hooker has to listen for it.
- Wheel the pack a few degrees on the machine and ask them to square up.
- No.8 pick-up as the alternative to DSP — later in the block, not tonight.

**Adaptations:**
- Machine in use, or the walk won't fit: run part 4 unopposed against a coach-held shield wall on the pitch.
- Forwards short: a back in the second row, or a group of two in part 3.
- Running long: cut the engage count in part 4, not parts 2 and 3.
- Anyone uncomfortable in the front row: move them, no discussion.
- Only one coach spare: run parts 2 and 4 whole-group and drop part 3 to two groups, front row and back five.

**Video example:** [The Scrum — Teenage Skills, Rugby Toolbox](https://www.rugbytoolbox.co.nz/training/technique/the-scrum-3)

### Exit kicks — technique, the channel, kick and chase (backs)

**Groups:** all backs — ~14. Pairs, then two groups of seven.

**Coaching Points:** ball at arm's length, point down and slightly in. **Drop it, don't throw it.** Strike with the laces, leg through the ball, follow through at the target. Eyes on the ball at the strike. **End over end is fine — distance and direction beat style.** Target is the **15m channel**, never the touchline.

**Setup:** the coned kicking channel, and a marked kicking spot at the near-third line. **A ball between two.** Under lights, so keep them low and long — nobody is catching a bomb out there. Three parts.

**Description:**

**1. Everyone kicks (~8 min).** Pairs, 15m apart across the square, punt and catch. Step back 5m each time three in a row are caught clean. Every player kicks — no watching.

**2. Into the channel (~8 min).** Two groups of seven, two kicking spots on the third line, the rest of each group standing in the channel as targets and retrievers. Five kicks each, count how many land in the channel, swap. Call your score out.

**3. Kick and chase (~8 min).** The picture, off both sides.

1. **9 passes** to the kicker, standing inside our own third.
2. **Kicker exits** into the channel.
3. **Three chasers go with the kick** — up together, nobody ahead of the kicker.
4. **A receiver fields it** and runs. Chasers touch him.

Reset fast and rotate the kicker every rep — high-rep, not a walk-through.

**Progressions:**
- Kicker has to call **Eagle** before the ball leaves 9 (see `playbook.md`).
- A defender on the kicker, closing, so it has to be quick.
- Two receivers back, so the chase has to pick the right man.

**Adaptations:**
- Kicks going nowhere: shorten everything — pairs at 10m, and make part 2 the whole block.
- Ball unfindable in the dark: bring part 2's kicking spot in, and put two retrievers in the channel.
- Coach short: run parts 1 and 2 as one group, and skip part 3.
- Anyone who cannot get it off the ground: drop kicks from the hand off a bounce for a few reps, then back to the punt.

### Game 2 — same game, exit kicks live

**Groups:** one game, the same two teams as Game 1. Same pitch, same bibs.

**Coaching Points:** **Bang** and the calls stay the first job. With the ball in your own third, the exit is on. Chase together or don't chase. Coach it in the stoppages.

**Setup:** the same square, the same teams, the same conditions still on — **nothing gets re-coned or re-picked.** Nineteen minutes, with a **team swap and water at about 20:00**.

**Description:** everything from Game 1, plus the kick.

**The exit condition:**

- **Kicking is allowed from inside your own third only.** Nowhere else on the pitch.
- **One point** for a kick that lands or is fielded beyond the far third line.
- **A second point** if the chase touches the receiver before he gets back into the middle third.
- **Play carries on from the catch** — no restart.

**Progressions:**
- **Heard it** stays on from Game 1 if it went on there.
- Step the last ten minutes up to Ruckly-Touch if the group is sharp and the light is holding (see `activities.md`).
- A point for a chase line with nobody offside across a whole phase.

**Adaptations:**
- Nobody kicking: take the condition off for two phases and hand them a free exit from a tap.
- Everybody kicking, and the game has stopped: one exit per team per two minutes.
- Ball lost in the dark repeatedly: drop the kick condition and finish on Game 1's rules.
- Game flat: swap a pair over at the water break.

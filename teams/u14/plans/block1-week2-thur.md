## Activities

### Essential skills — passing (Crazy Passing)

**Groups:** two sets — three lines of four or five in each.

**Coaching Points:** elbow up, hands out, catch early. Call for it before it comes. With two balls, know where both are without looking.

**Setup:** three lines (A, B, C) spaced for passing, two sets side by side in the near end. Split the squad however you like — this one does not use the night's teams. Full detail in `activities.md`.

**Description:** the ball zig-zags down the lines, each player following their pass to the back of the line they passed to. Second ball in once the pattern is clean.

**Progressions:**
- Second ball in.
- Widen the spacing.
- Time a full circuit, then beat it.

**Adaptations:**
- Pattern breaking down: take the second ball out and rebuild.
- Passes dropping short: shorten the spacing.
- Poor form in two or three: a spare coach takes them off the side, in pairs, while the drill runs.

### Hooker throw-in — five minutes at a pop (hookers)

**Groups:** hookers only — in twos or threes, one coach.

**Coaching Points:** same routine every throw. Ball back **behind the head**, both hands, step into it, follow through **pointing at the target**. Eyes on the target.

**Setup:** the throwing spot and the coned line at the posts. Out of Crazy Passing for five minutes, back in, out for five more.

**Description:**

1. **Out (~5 min)** — two minutes of the Cross-Bar Challenge (see `activities.md`), then throw to the coned line. One throws, one receives at the called cone, one returns balls. Rotate every throw.
2. **Back into the passing (~3 min).**
3. **Out again (~5 min)** — the coned line only. Score out of ten each; beat the first stint.

**Progressions:**
- Receiver calls front, middle or back as the hooker starts the routine.
- A defender's hands up in front of the receiver, to force height.
- Call it with a **B/W/G** word (see `playbook.md`) instead of front, middle, back.

**Adaptations:**
- Only two hookers: one throws, one receives, the coach returns balls.
- Throws falling short: move the spot in.
- Throws landing clean every time: back to the 15m cone only.
- A non-hooker keen: a few throws each, as cover.

### Vocal warm-up — everyone shouts

**Groups:** whole squad, one circle.

**Coaching Points:** everyone at once, loudly. Two minutes, no more, then straight into the drill.

**Setup:** one circle, no equipment, coach in the middle.

**Description:** three steps, two minutes.

1. **All together** — on three, everyone shouts something daft. Twice, second one louder.
2. **Teams against each other** — the two teams out-shout each other. Judge it badly and obviously.
3. **Round the circle** — own name in turn, fast, no gaps.

Then stop, and don't explain it.

**Adaptations:**
- **Offer the opt-out to the whole squad before you start** — clap or stamp instead of shouting.
- Step 3 dying: back to step 1 and finish there.
- Something different to shout from last week.

### Outside shoulder — mirror, 2 v 2, 3 v 3

**Groups:** pairs, then fours, then sixes — spread across both small pitches.

**Coaching Points:** **nose on his outside shoulder** — the one nearer the touchline. Square, inside foot forward, short steps. Close the space, then match his speed. Call your man.

**Setup:** the two small pitches, no extra cones. Walk it, then jog, then run. Touch only.

**Description:**

1. **Mirror (~3 min).** Pairs, 5m apart, a ball with the attacker. Coach names the touchline. Attacker steps side to side across a 5m strip; defender keeps his nose on the attacker's outside shoulder. Swap every 30 seconds.
2. **2 v 2 (~4 min).** Half a small pitch's width, from the start line. Coach rolls the ball to either attacker. Both defenders go up together, each on the outside shoulder of his man. Attack goes for the try line; a touch ends it. Swap after four.
3. **3 v 3 (~5 min).** The same, three a side, feed from either side. Defence calls **Bang** on the feed.

**Progressions:**
- Walk, jog, run.
- In the mirror, the attacker may step inside once — the defender stays outside.
- 3 v 3 becomes 4 v 3 (see Blitz Defence 4 v 3 in `activities.md`).

**Adaptations:**
- Defenders overrunning their man: back to walking pace.
- Pairs badly mismatched for pace: swap partners.
- Defence ball-watching: the attacker calls a number, the defender shouts it back.

### Outside-shoulder game — fed from either side

**Groups:** two pitches — 7 v 7 on each, the defence playing six and rotating one out every rep.

**Coaching Points:** **Bang** on the feed, and the line goes together. **Nose on his outside shoulder**, working in from the side the ball came. Call your man before you move. Show him back inside.

**Setup:** one small pitch each. Attack on the start line; defence 10m in front of it. **A coach or spare player on each touchline at the start line, with balls.** Touch only — a touch ends the rep. Twenty minutes; swap attack and defence every three.

**Description:**

1. **A feeder passes to the nearest attacker** — either side, no pattern.
2. **Defence calls Bang and goes up**, each defender picking up the outside shoulder of his man.
3. **Attack has one go at the try line.** Try — a point to attack. Touch — a point to defence.
4. **Reset fast** — defence back to 10m, one defender rotates out, next feed.

**Progressions:**
- **Gate** — the defence starts behind its try line and comes through the gate on the feed, so the line has to form on the move.
- **Colour cone** — the feeder calls a colour as he feeds; every defender touches a cone of that colour before joining the line.
- Defence starts facing its own try line and turns on the feed.
- The feeder shows the ball one side and feeds from the other.
- Two touches: on the first, the carrier goes down and presents, and the attack plays on.

**Adaptations:**
- Attack scoring every rep: shorten the pitch, or give the defence its seventh man back.
- Defence winning every rep: widen the pitch, or play 7 v 5.
- Defence all drifting to the ball: feed from the same side twice, then switch.
- Resets too slow: a second ball ready with each feeder.

### Scrum — body position and the feed, no machine (forwards)

**Groups:** all forwards plus both 9s — ~14. Front row and 9s in one group, the back five in the other.

**Coaching Points:** **shoulders above hips, always.** Straight back, chin off the chest, eyes up. **Props — once you're set, your feet stay where they are.** Hooker strikes **on the feed**. No push tonight.

**Setup:** on the pitch, next to the small pitches. **No machine, no engage, no pack against pack.** Two parts.

**Description:**

**1. Body position — all forwards together (~5 min).**

1. Drop into the tower of power on a call, hold for ten seconds, get it checked, reset. Five or six.
2. **Bear crawl** — flat back, hips low, head up. Out and back, freezing into the position on a call.

**2. Two groups (~8 min).** One coach each.

| Group | Who | The job |
|---|---|---|
| **a** | **Front row + both 9s** | Bind standing, then crouch and hold the **set** position — **props' feet planted, nobody stepping back**. Coach checks feet on every set. Then 9 feeds on the one timing word, hooker strikes. Both 9s rotate through. |
| **b** | **Back five — locks, 6, 7, 8** | The binds, standing: locks' head into the gap, inside arm through onto the shorts; 6 and 7 onto the locks; 8 between the locks. Crouch and hold the shape, no push. Then 8 controlling a rolled ball at his feet. |

**Progressions:**
- Front row holds the set for longer, feet still planted.
- 9 varies the timing so the hooker has to listen for it.

**Adaptations:**
- Only one coach spare: part 2 as one group, front row working and the back five binding behind them.
- Forwards short: a back into the second row.
- Anyone uncomfortable in the front row: move them, no discussion.

### Exit kicks — technique, the channel, kick and chase (backs)

**Groups:** all backs — ~14. Pairs, then two groups of seven.

**Coaching Points:** ball at arm's length, point down and slightly in. **Drop it, don't throw it.** Strike with the laces, leg through the ball, follow through at the target. Eyes on the ball at the strike. **End over end is fine — distance and direction beat style.** Target is the **15m channel**, never the touchline.

**Setup:** the kicking channel, and a marked kicking spot at the near-third line. **A ball between two.** Under lights, so keep them low and long. Three short parts.

**Description:**

**1. Everyone kicks (~4 min).** Pairs, 15m apart, punt and catch. Step back 5m each time three in a row are caught clean.

**2. Into the channel (~4 min).** Two groups of seven, two kicking spots on the third line, the rest of each group in the channel as targets and retrievers. Three kicks each, count how many land in the channel. Call your score out.

**3. Kick and chase (~5 min).**

1. **9 passes** to the kicker, standing inside our own third.
2. **Kicker exits** into the channel.
3. **Three chasers go with the kick** — up together, nobody ahead of the kicker.
4. **A receiver fields it** and runs. Chasers touch him.

Rotate the kicker every rep.

**Progressions:**
- Kicker has to call **Eagle** before the ball leaves 9 (see `playbook.md`).
- A defender on the kicker, closing, so it has to be quick.

**Adaptations:**
- Kicks going nowhere: pairs at 10m, and make part 2 the whole block.
- Ball unfindable in the dark: bring the kicking spot in, two retrievers in the channel.
- Coach short: parts 1 and 2 only.

### Game — touch, Bang and the outside shoulder

**Groups:** one game, the night's two teams whole.

**Coaching Points:** **Bang**, and the line goes together. **Nose on his outside shoulder.** Call your man. The shuffling retreat. Coach it in the stoppages, one or two players at a time.

**Setup:** the full square. **Presentation Touch** — on a touch the carrier goes down, turns, and presents long (see `activities.md`). Every restart is a **tap** on the spot. Fifteen minutes, straight through.

**Description:** conditions added in this order and left on:

1. **Bang** — the blitz trigger (see `playbook.md`).
2. **Springbok Touch** — first receiver caught still holding the ball is a turnover, taken as a tap to the defence (see `activities.md`).

**Progressions:**
- **Heard it** — the turnover only counts if **Bang** came from the line's nominated caller and the line went together.
- **Exit kicks live** — kicking allowed from inside your own third only; a point for a kick landing beyond the far third line.
- *If the talk has gone flat:* two minutes of **silence** — no defensive talk — then take it off.

**Adaptations:**
- Attack getting out too easily: bring the touchlines in a third. Defence smothering everything: widen it back out.
- One team running away with it: swap a pair over.
- Numbers low: one team of whoever is there against the rest, still one game.

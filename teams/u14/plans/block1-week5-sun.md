## Activities

### Essential skills — passing (Crazy Passing)

**Groups:** two sets — three lines in each.

**Coaching Points:** **pass in front of him, not at him.** **Hands up before the ball comes** — a target, fingers spread. Call for it.

**Setup:** three cones in a line per group, 5m apart, two groups side by side between the try line and the 22. Split it however you like.

**Description:**

1. Ball zig-zags down the lines, each player following their pass to the back of the line they passed to.
2. **Receivers jog onto it** — nobody catches standing still.
3. Second ball in once the pattern is clean.
4. **Last three minutes: a football in place of one ball.**

**Progressions:**
- Widen the spacing.
- Football in place of both balls.
- Pass off the weaker hand only.

**Adaptations:**
- Pattern breaking down: take the second ball out and rebuild.
- Passes going at the chest: receiver stands still, passer aims a metre ahead of him.
- Casual hands in two or three: a spare coach takes them off the side, in pairs, while the drill runs.

### Contact warm-up (whole squad)

**Groups:** whole squad, in pairs matched by size.

**Coaching Points:** in the pummel — chest up, back flat, **head to the side**, hips low, arms working continuously. In Wrist Wrestle — stay low, feet moving, win it with body position.

**Setup:** everyone in pairs, **matched by size**. No equipment. Straight into the boxes and the Continuous Attack game after it.

**Description:** the standard two minutes — demo not needed.

1. **Get low (~30s)** — on the spot, feet moving, drop into the tackle-ready position. Five or six reps.
2. **Pummelling (~45s)** — pairs chest to chest **on knees**, one arm inside and one outside, both swimming the outside arm inside on the call.
3. **Wrist Wrestle (~45s)** — standing, same-wrist grips locked, free hand behind the back, slap with the held hand. A point per clean touch, then reset.

Rules unchanged: pummelling is an arm battle — no lifting, no takedowns, no twisting a neck. Wrist Wrestle targets **shoulder or upper arm only**, and stops the moment a grip goes. **Anyone who would rather not play the slapping game pummels again instead — offer it to the whole squad up front.**

**Adaptations:**
- Anyone new or back from injury: start at stage 1, join the boxes when a coach says so.
- Badly mismatched pair: break the three and rotate.

### Four Corners — 2 v 2, then 3 v 3

**Groups:** one team at a time — two boxes, seven or eight in each. 2 v 2 for about three minutes, then 3 v 3. Swap with the Continuous Attack game at the water break, +25.

**Coaching Points:** **ball away on two.** Support arrives before the second defender, cleaner **low and through, past the ball**. Carrier goes forward, presents long. Tackler: **low, shoulder contact, squeeze**, head to the side, feet driving.

**Setup:** **a box per coach — a box without a coach does not run.** 10m square, two boxes side by side. **Attack takes the two near cones, defence the two far cones**; the line between the far pair is the **try line**. **3 v 3 start lines: 3m back from each end.** Everyone else waits outside the box. Contact below the armpits, nobody plays the ball off their feet (see `laws.md`).

**Description:**

**2 v 2 (~3 min)**

1. **Four players on the cones.** The attackers pass between them, feet on their cones.
2. **Someone outside the box shouts "Go"** — no warning.
3. Everyone live. Attack runs at the far line; defence comes off its cones.
4. Carrier tackled. Second attacker clears, second defender contests **only if the ball is there**.
5. **Coach counts "one, two" out loud from the tackle.** **Attack wins:** a score, or the ball secured on two. **Defence wins:** a turnover or a slow ball.
6. **Rotate every rep** — attack to defence, two in.

**3 v 3 (the rest)**

1. **Both threes start on their start lines, 3m back from the box.** Attack passes along its line.
2. "Go" — both threes run in. Live from the call.
3. Carrier tackled. One attacker clears; **the third plays 9 — ball away on two.**
4. Defence: one tackles, one contests **only if the ball is there**, one guards the side of the ruck.
5. **Rotate every rep** — attack to defence, defence out, the waiting players in.

**Progressions:**
- **Mixed starts** — one attacker and one defender on each cone, so the read is diagonal.
- Call a name instead of "Go" — that player has to be the carrier.
- Attack must make one pass after the call before contact.

**Adaptations:**
- Attack scoring every rep: shorten the box, or bring the start lines in. Defence winning everything: lengthen it.
- Too few waiting for a 3 v 3 rotation: swap attack and defence each rep, two in for the carrier and the tackler.
- Anyone sore from Thursday or school: outside the box as the caller.
- Ground hard: make it a hold rather than a tackle.
- **Match by size**, box by box.
- A coach short: one box, rotate every rep so nobody stands long.

### Continuous Attack — overload

**Groups:** one team at a time — attack two up, about 8 v 6. **A defence drill.** Swap attack and defence halfway. Swap with Four Corners at the water break, +25.

**Coaching Points:** **coach the defence, not the attack — set before the ball:** numbered, pointing, **outside shoulder**. **Up together on Bang.** **Left side, left foot forward; right side, right foot forward.** **Sprint back facing** — shuffle, never turn and run.

**Setup:** between the 22 and halfway, right side — **25m wide, 22m long**. Attack's start line at one end, defence's try line at the other. **Coach on the touchline at halfway with a pile of balls.** Touch.

**Description:**

1. Attack lines up on its start line. Coach passes a ball in. Attack goes for the try line.
2. On a touch the carrier presents; support picks and plays.
3. **Defence wins on the third touch**, a dropped ball or a forward pass.
4. **After every try or defensive win:** attack retreats behind its start line. Defence sprints back to its try line and sets.
5. **Coach injects the next ball the moment the attack is behind the line.** Defence that isn't set plays anyway.
6. Keep it running. **Coaches watch the defence only.**

**Progressions:**
- **Coach injects the next ball the moment the rep ends** — attack plays from wherever it is.
- **Silent defence** for one minute, then the talk back on.

**Adaptations:**
- Defence swamped every rep: cut the overload to one, or narrow the pitch.
- Attack never scoring: widen the pitch, or defence wins on the fourth touch.
- Defence walking back: inject the ball sooner.

### Springbok Touch — Coach's Bag

**Groups:** the two teams, full half.

**Coaching Points:** **up on Bang — get to the first receiver.** **Eyes up: crossbar, touchline, call it (CTC).** Tell the scorer what the item was.

**Setup:** the full half — the try line and the cone line 22m from halfway are the two try lines. Touch. **Four coaches spread round the pitch, each with a bag of items** — not clustered.

**Description:**

1. Touch rugby. On a touch the carrier presents; support picks and plays.
2. **Defence touches the first receiver with the ball in his hands: turnover.**
3. **Coach's Bag:** every 30–45 seconds a coach holds an item up for a few seconds, then puts it away. Vary who shows it.
4. **A try counts only if the scorer names the last item shown.** No name, no try — restart.
5. Restarts: a tap from halfway.

**Progressions:**
- Show an item only while play is live and fast.
- Defence names the item to keep a turnover.
- Two coaches show different items at once.

**Adaptations:**
- Nobody seeing the items: hold them up longer, stand closer.
- Tries disallowed every time: a team-mate can answer for the scorer.
- Defence never reaching the first receiver: narrow the pitch.

### Scrum — front row and 9s

**Groups:** both teams' front rows and both 9s — two front rows, with Jeff.

**Coaching Points:** **hooker taps when he's ready — 9 feeds on the tap, no call.** **9: eyes on the hooker's hand, feed straight, fast, to the strike foot.** **Hooker strikes on the feed, not before.** Loosehead binds on, not under; feet adjust on the set, then stay.

**Setup:** the front-row mark. The two front rows face each other. Jeff makes the safety call. A scrum going down: everybody stops.

**Description:**

1. **Set (~3 min).** Crouch — bind — set, no push. Check binds and feet every time.
2. **Tap and feed (~6 min).** Front row v front row, no push. Hooker taps, 9 feeds, hooker strikes. Ball goes back through the tunnel. Swap the feed every rep. Both 9s rotate.
3. **Vary it (~6 min).** Hooker holds the tap a second or two — 9 waits for it. Half pressure for the last few.

**Progressions:**
- Half pressure, then the hooker has to strike under it.
- Hooker taps early or late — 9 must wait for it.
- Opposing hooker strikes for it — both sides contesting the ball.

**Adaptations:**
- Feed before the tap: 9 says "tap" out loud as he sees it, then feeds.
- Feet going back on the set: shorter sets, check feet before any feed.
- Only one front row out: on the scrum machine, Jeff feeding.
- Anyone uncomfortable in the front row: move him, no discussion.

### Scrum — back row and Hammer

**Groups:** both teams' locks and back rows, with Tiggy.

**Coaching Points:** **8: stop it with the inside of the foot, keep it at the base.** **Flankers: stay bound until the ball is out, then break.** **Hammer: 8 picks blind, low and straight. Blind-side flanker on his hip. Second carry blind, straight off the ruck.** Know where you need to be before the ball comes in.

**Setup:** the back-row mark. **Two ruck shields held upright side by side** by the waiting players — the front row. Two locks bind on them; 8 binds between the locks; flankers on the locks. Tiggy at 9 with the balls, rolling it through.

**Description:**

1. **Control it (~5 min).** Tiggy rolls the ball through. 8 stops it with the inside of his foot and holds it at the base. Vary the pace and the line. Flankers stay bound until Tiggy takes it, then break.
2. **Hammer (~10 min).** Tiggy calls **Hammer** before the roll.
   1. 8 controls it, picks, carries **blind**. Goes to ground, presents.
   2. **Blind-side flanker** on his hip — clears ruck 1.
   3. Tiggy plays 9 off ruck 1 to **the second carrier, also blind** — the open-side flanker, the nearer lock on his hip.
   4. The other lock folds round to the blind side for the next ruck.
3. Swap which side is blind every rep. Swap the 8s and flankers between the teams.

**Progressions:**
- A shield-holder defends the blind side — the carriers have to win the contact.
- Third carry, back to the open side.
- 9 calls **DSP** or **Hammer** — back row has to read it.

**Adaptations:**
- Ball squirting out: roll it slower, 8 standing still.
- Flanker breaking early: Tiggy calls "out" — flanker goes on the call.
- Second carrier late: walk the Hammer first, then at pace.
- Only one 8 out: a flanker or lock takes the other turn.

### Backs — attack off the scrum

**Groups:** all backs — attack against a defence of four, swapping every few reps. **Bev with the attack, Steve with the defence.**

**Coaching Points:** **time the run — arrive at pace as 10 gets it, not before.** **10: read the defenders' shoulders, pick the runner who's free.** **12, 13 and 15 out the back — every line live, every rep.** Hands up, ball in front of the receiver.

**Setup:** the backs scrum mark on the 22, right 15m line. A spare back at 9 with the balls. Attack lined up off the scrum, playing towards the left touchline. Defence starts 10m back. Touch.

**Description:**

1. 9 clears it to 10.
2. **12 and 13 run hard lines, 15 runs out the back.** Wingers hold their width.
3. **10 picks: 12, 13, or behind them to 15** — whoever the defence leaves.
4. **Steve sets the defenders' shoulders before each rep**, quietly, so 10 gets a different picture.
5. On a touch, the rep ends. Reset at the mark, next ball straight away.
6. Swap attack and defence every four or five reps.

**Progressions:**
- Defence goes live on **Bang** — no set picture.
- Defence of five.
- Second phase — on a touch, present, 9 to it, go again.

**Adaptations:**
- Runners arriving early: start them deeper.
- 10 always giving it to the same man: Steve sets the defence to take him away.
- Attack scoring every rep: one more defender.

### Main game — scrum restarts

**Groups:** the two teams, full half, 15 a side if the numbers allow.

**Coaching Points:** **backs set before the ball leaves the base.** **Defence set before the feed** — numbered, outside shoulder, feet right. **Up on Bang.** **Every scrum to us: hooker taps, 9 calls it — backs or Hammer.**

**Setup:** the full half. **Springbok Presentation Touch** — on a touch the carrier goes to ground and presents long; first receiver caught holding the ball is a turnover. Scrums **bound and set, no push**, ball to the attack.

**Description:**

1. **First scrum each way: walked through, unopposed** — 9 calls backs one way, Hammer the other. Then live.
2. **Every restart is a scrum to the attack** — after a try, a turnover or the ball going dead. Into touch: scrum on the 15m.
3. Hooker taps, 9 feeds, **8 controls it at the base**. 9 calls it: **backs** or **Hammer**.
4. Off the backs: **10 picks the runner** — 12, 13, or 15 out the back.
5. Defence sets before the feed. The coach standing back calls what wasn't set, to one or two players, at the next scrum.
6. **Last seven minutes:** pick-and-go on at the ruck. **Defence puts a guard either side.**

**Progressions:**
- **Pick-and-go on** at the ruck — defence guards either side, and commits a second body only when it's coming round the same side.
- Two-second ruck count: any ruck over two seconds is a turnover.
- A try off the first phase of a scrum is worth two.

**Adaptations:**
- Defence not setting: referee holds the feed until it is, and says why once.
- 9 not calling: no call, no feed.
- Attack getting out too easily: bring the touchlines in.
- Numbers under 22: narrow the pitch.

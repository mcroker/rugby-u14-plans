## Activities

### Essential skills — passing (Crazy Passing)

**Groups:** two sets — three lines in each.

**Coaching Points:** **hands up before the ball comes** — a target, fingers spread. **Catch it early, in front of the chest**, not into the body. Pass in front of him, not at him.

**Setup:** three cones in a line per group, 5m apart, two groups side by side in the near end. Split it however you like.

**Description:**

1. Ball zig-zags down the lines, each player following their pass to the back of the line they passed to.
2. **Receivers jog onto it** — nobody catches standing still.
3. Second ball in once the pattern is clean.
4. **Last four minutes: cones in to 2m — pod distance.** Short, flat, quick. Still two balls.

**Progressions:**
- At 2m: receivers come onto it at pace, hands up early.
- Widen back to 5m and add a third ball.

**Adaptations:**
- Pattern breaking down: take the second ball out and rebuild.
- Knock-ons at 2m: slow to a walk, hands up before the pass is thrown.
- Casual hands in two or three: a spare coach takes them off the side, in pairs, while the drill runs.

### Hookers — lineout throw, 1 on 1

**Groups:** the hookers — one each with Jeff and Tiggy. Back into the squad for Continuous Attack at +15.

**Coaching Points:** **same routine every throw** — feet, grip, ball position. Ball back **behind the head**, both hands, thumbs driving through. Step into it; follow through **pointing at the target**. Eyes on the target, not the ball.

**Setup:** a cone as the throwing mark on a touchline, the coach as the target. A few balls each.

**Description:**

1. Coach stands at the front of the line, 5m. Five throws, coach catches above the head.
2. Back to the middle, 10m. Five throws.
3. Coach calls front or middle as the hooker starts his routine. Count out of ten.

**Progressions:**
- Coach calls the target late, after the hooker has set.
- Back of the line, 13–15m.

**Adaptations:**
- Ball not reaching: shorten the distance.
- Throw going wide: back to 5m, check the follow-through.

### Continuous Attack — rolling ball

**Groups:** one pitch per team — attack two up inside the team, about 7 v 5 with the 9s out. Swap attack and defence halfway.

**Coaching Points:** **defence: sprint back facing, set, number up, up together on Bang.** Shuffle back — never turn and run. **Attack: ball away in two** — carrier presents long, support picks.

**Setup:** the two small pitches in the far half. Attack's start line at one end, defence's try line at the other. **Coach on the touchline at halfway with a pile of balls.** Touch.

**Description:**

1. Attack lines up on its start line. Coach passes a ball in. Attack goes for the try line.
2. On a touch the carrier presents; support picks and plays.
3. **Defence wins on the third touch**, a dropped ball or a forward pass.
4. **The moment a rep ends, the coach passes the next ball in to the attack wherever it is.** No retreating to the start line.
5. **Defence works back to its try line and sets** while the ball is coming — a defence that isn't set plays anyway.
6. Keep it running. **Coaches watch the defence.**

**Progressions:**
- **Ruck over two seconds is a defensive win.**
- **Silent defence** for one minute, then the talk back on.

**Adaptations:**
- Defence swamped every rep: cut the overload to one, or narrow the pitch.
- Attack never scoring: widen the pitch, or defence wins on the fourth touch.
- Flagging: shorter reps, swap attack and defence more often.

### 9s — lineout ball on the run, 1 on 1

**Groups:** the 9s, with Nick. Back into Continuous Attack at +25.

**Coaching Points:** **go as the ball leaves the jumper's hands** — not on the throw. **Hands up early, catch it moving.** Pass on the first step after the catch — no stopping.

**Setup:** Nick on a cone as the jumper. A cone 3m back and towards the tail for the 9's start. A cone 5m beyond 10's position as the target — where 6 and 8 would be.

**Description:**

1. Nick holds the ball up at catch height and pops it down.
2. 9 goes as it leaves Nick's hands, takes it on the run, passes to the target cone.
3. Five each side, then Nick varies the timing of the pop — 9 watches the hands, not a count.

**Progressions:**
- Nick varies the timing of the pop.
- Nick moves along the line — front, middle — and the 9 adjusts his start.

**Adaptations:**
- Arriving before the ball: start further back.
- Fumbling on the run: walk it, then jog, then run.
- Running catch not working at all: switch to passing off the floor — feet to the ball, sweep it, no back-lift.

### Gain Line Touch — with Coach's Bag

**Groups:** two games, 7 v 7 — each team halved, bibs v non-bibs on each pitch.

**Coaching Points:** **defence: work back, get onside, set, up together on Bang** — stop him short. **Attack: ball away in two, carry hard and straight** over the line. **Everyone: eyes up at every breakdown — CTC.**

**Setup:** the two small pitches. Touch. **Referee level with the ruck**, calling every touch. **A coach on each touchline with the score-card numbers**, moving up and down.

**Description:**

1. Touch. On a touch the carrier goes to ground and presents long; support picks and plays.
2. **The gain line runs across the pitch through the last ruck.** First phase: through the coach's feed cone.
3. **Every touch, the referee calls "Over" or "Stopped".**
   - **Over** — touched past the gain line: **a point to the attack.**
   - **Stopped** — touched short of it: **a point to the defence.**
4. **Defence onside** — behind the ball — before it comes up. Offside: automatic Over.
5. **Sets of six touches**, then the ball changes over. A try is worth three and ends the set.
6. **Ruck over two seconds, dropped ball or forward pass: the set ends** and the ball changes over.
7. **Coach's Bag:** at every breakdown the touchline coach holds up a number.
8. **A point only counts if the player the referee names — on the side that won it — calls the number from the previous breakdown.** Wrong or no answer: no point. Play doesn't stop.
9. Touchline coach keeps the score — a clicker for each team.

**Progressions:**
- **A try after three rucks counts double.**
- Stopped twice in a row: the set ends.

**Adaptations:**
- Calls being argued: drop a cone at each ruck.
- Attack going nowhere but narrow: a try outside the last cone scores five.
- Attack over every time: defence gets a point for any touch on the gain line.
- Flagging: sets of four.
- Nobody remembering the last number: name the current one instead.
- Nobody seeing the numbers: hold them up longer, stand closer.

### Main game — lineout restarts

**Groups:** 14 v 14, full width.

**Coaching Points:** **keep it alive past the lineout** — ball away in two, every ruck. **Defence: work back facing, set before the ball, up on Bang.** **Lineout to us: 5-man Rhino.** 9 runs onto the ball off the top.

**Setup:** the whole pitch, **Springbok Presentation Touch** — on a touch the carrier goes to ground and presents long; first receiver caught holding the ball is a turnover. **Ruck over two seconds is a turnover.** Lineouts uncontested, five in the line.

**Description:**

1. **Every restart is a lineout to the attack** — after a try, a turnover or the ball going dead. Alternate the touchline.
2. Lineout: **5-man Rhino** — ruck 1, ruck 2, backs wide.
3. **Defending the lineout:** four in our line, the spare stands deep opposite 10 and closes him on the pass.
4. Defence sets before the throw. The coach standing back calls who didn't work back, to one or two players, at the next restart.
5. Play to a score, a turnover or dead ball. Next lineout.

**Progressions:**
- **A try after three or more rucks counts double.**
- Stopped short on every phase: a point to the defence, as in Gain Line Touch.

**Adaptations:**
- Lineouts slowing it down: tap restarts for five minutes.
- Defence not setting: referee holds the throw until it is, once.
- Attack getting out too easily: bring the touchlines in.
- Numbers under 22: narrow the pitch, three in the lineout.

## Activities

### Essential skills — passing (Crazy Passing)

**Groups:** three lines — 4, 4 and 3.

**Coaching Points:** **hands up before the ball comes** — a target, fingers spread. **Catch it early**, in front of the chest. Pass in front of him, not at him.

**Setup:** three cones in a line, 5m apart.

**Description:**

1. Ball zig-zags down the lines, each player following their pass to the back of the line they passed to.
2. Receivers jog onto it.
3. Second ball in once the pattern is clean.
4. **Last three minutes: cones in to 2m — pod distance.**
5. **Last minute:** a coach holds a shield 10m in front. On the last pass he calls **"Bang"** — all eleven up to him together, in a line.

**Progressions:**
- On Bang: the line stops 1m short, set, inside foot forward.
- Third ball.

**Adaptations:**
- Pattern breaking down: take the second ball out.
- Knock-ons at 2m: slow to a walk.

### Set! — defensive set-up

**Groups:** 6 defend, 5 attack. Swap every three calls.

**Coaching Points:** **number off, point at your man, outside shoulder.** **Left side, left foot forward; right side, right foot forward.** Talk — loud.

**Setup:** the game pitch. Touch. A pile of balls with the coach.

**Description:**

1. Coach calls a restart and a spot: **"Scrum, left, 15m"**, **"Lineout, right"**, **"Kick-off"**.
2. Attack sets up for it. Defence has **five seconds to be set**.
3. **Coach counts out loud, then checks the line.** Anyone not set: the attack gets a free play — one more phase.
4. One phase of touch. Defence up on **Bang**.
5. Next call, somewhere else on the pitch.

**Scrum calls:** the attack stands where the backs would off a scrum, with a coach as the scrum. **Defence nominates the blind side — someone points at it.** Attack goes blind every third call.

**Kick-off calls:** **all six in the line before the kick.** Count who's out of it.

**Progressions:**
- Four seconds.
- Attack picks the side; the defence reads it.

**Adaptations:**
- Defence never set: walk the first two through.
- Attack scoring every phase: drop the free play.

### Contact warm-up (whole squad)

**Groups:** whole squad, in pairs matched by size. Odd one out with a coach.

**Coaching Points:** in the pummel — chest up, back flat, **head to the side**, hips low, arms working continuously. In Wrist Wrestle — stay low, feet moving, win it with body position.

**Setup:** pairs, **matched by size**. No equipment.

**Description:** the standard two minutes.

1. **Get low (~30s)** — on the spot, feet moving, drop into the tackle-ready position. Five or six reps.
2. **Pummelling (~45s)** — pairs chest to chest **on knees**, one arm inside and one outside, both swimming the outside arm inside on the call.
3. **Wrist Wrestle (~45s)** — standing, same-wrist grips locked, free hand behind the back, slap with the held hand. A point per clean touch, then reset.

Pummelling: no lifting, no takedowns, no twisting a neck. Wrist Wrestle: **shoulder or upper arm only**, stops the moment a grip goes. **Anyone who would rather not play the slapping game pummels again instead — offer it up front.**

### 1 v 1 tackle channel

**Groups:** two channels — 6 and 5, a coach each.

**Coaching Points:** **low — chop.** **Cheek to cheek** — head to the side, behind the carrier. **Squeeze** with both arms. **Feet keep driving** through contact — don't plant.

**Setup:** two channels, 5m wide, 10m long. Carrier at one end, tackler at the other.

**Description:**

1. **Walk-through:** carrier walks in, tackler steps in, cheek on his backside, squeezes. Three each side.
2. **Jog:** carrier jogs straight, tackler goes to ground with him. Three each side.
3. **Live in the channel:** carrier can step once. **Tackler wins if the tackle is made before halfway.**
4. Tackler becomes the carrier. Back of the line.

**Progressions:**
- Tackler starts on his knees, then on his feet.
- Channel widened to 7m.

**Adaptations:**
- **Pair by size.** Swap anyone badly mismatched.
- Head on the wrong side: back to walking.
- Anyone not tackling today: carrier only, then caller in Four Corners.

### Four Corners — 2 v 2

**Groups:** two boxes of four. The other three call "Go" and rotate in.

**Coaching Points:** **defence: front-on tackle — low, shoulder contact, squeeze, feet driving.** Second defender only goes for the ball if it's there; otherwise stays on his feet. **Attack: ball away on two.**

**Setup:** two boxes, 10m square. Attack on the near pair of cones, defence on the far pair. The far line is the try line.

**Description:**

1. Attackers pass between themselves, feet on their cones.
2. **A caller outside the box shouts "Go"** — no warning.
3. Everyone is live. Attack runs at the far line; defence comes off its cones.
4. Carrier is tackled. **The other two join the breakdown.**
5. **Attack wins by scoring. Defence wins by winning the turnover.**
6. Rotate: attack becomes defence, two from outside come in. Keep it moving.

**Progressions:**
- **Ball away on two** — the coach counts from the tackle; missing it is a defensive win.
- **Mixed corners** — one attacker and one defender on each cone.
- Call a name instead of "Go".

**Adaptations:**
- Attack scoring every rep: shorten the box. Defence winning everything: lengthen it.
- Ground hard: hold rather than tackle.
- **Pair by size.**
- **The caller varies the delay.** A different caller each rotation.

### Continuous Defence

**Groups:** 5 defend in the middle, 3 attack from each end. Rotate the five every three minutes.

**Coaching Points:** **turn, sprint back, set, face** — before the next ball comes. **Shuffle back facing, never turn and run** once the ball is coming. Number off and point every time.

**Setup:** the Continuous Defence pitch. A pile of balls at each end. Touch.

**Description:**

1. Three attackers come from one end. Defence stops them — touch, then the carrier presents and the next attacker picks.
2. **Defence wins on the second touch**, a dropped ball or a forward pass.
3. **The moment a rep ends, Andy sends the three from the other end.** Defence turns, sprints to its try line at that end, sets and comes up on **Bang**.
4. Attack that's just finished jogs back to its end and waits.
5. **The next wave starts before the defence is ready if anyone walks.**

**Progressions:**
- **Next wave starts as the last one ends** — no gap.
- Four attackers in each wave.
- Defence wins on the first touch.

**Adaptations:**
- Defence swamped: shrink the pitch, or waves of two.
- Defence never stretched: widen the pitch.
- Flagging: rotate every two minutes.

### Overload — 7 v 4, then 6 v 5

**Groups:** 7 attack, 4 defend. Then 6 attack, 5 defend. Rotate the defence every three minutes.

**Coaching Points:** **talk** — "I've got 3", "drift", "Bang". **Outside shoulder**, show him inside. **Touched defender: round the cone at a sprint and fill the gap — don't walk.**

**Setup:** the overload channel. A cone 5m behind the defence's start line, in the middle. Touch.

**Description:**

**7 v 4 (first seven minutes):**

1. Seven attack from one end. Four defend.
2. **Defence wins by stopping the attack before the far line** — a touch with the carrier short of halfway counts.
3. One phase. Reset.

**6 v 5 (last eight minutes):**

1. Six attack. Five defend.
2. **Attack gets three phases.** On a touch the carrier presents; support picks and plays.
3. **The touched defender goes round the cone behind the defence's line before rejoining.**
4. Defence resets onside and comes up on **Bang** off every ruck.
5. Defence wins on a stop at phase three, a dropped ball or a forward pass.

**Progressions:**
- **Any defender walking: the attack gets a fourth phase.**
- The two defenders nearest each ruck both go round the cone.

**Adaptations:**
- Defence never stopping it: narrow the channel to 12m.
- Attack never scoring: widen it to 20m.
- Attack always going wide: a try down the touchline side counts double.

### Gain Line Touch — Set! restarts

**Groups:** 6 v 5. One player is the joker and always attacks. Swap the joker each set.

**Coaching Points:** **defence: work back, get onside, set, up together on Bang** — stop him short. Number off and point at every restart. **Attack: ball away in two, carry hard and straight.**

**Setup:** the game pitch. Touch. **Andy level with the ruck**, calling every touch.

**Description:**

1. Touch. On a touch the carrier goes to ground and presents long; support picks and plays.
2. **The gain line runs across the pitch through the last ruck.**
3. **Every touch, Andy calls "Over" or "Stopped".**
   - **Over** — touched past the gain line: **a point to the attack.**
   - **Stopped** — touched short of it: **a point to the defence.**
4. **Defence onside before it comes up.** Offside: automatic Over.
5. **Sets of six touches**, then the ball changes over. A try is worth three and ends the set.
6. Ruck over two seconds, dropped ball or forward pass: the set ends.
7. **Every set starts from a Set! call** — scrum, lineout or kick-off, anywhere on the pitch. Five seconds to be set; anyone not set is an automatic Over.

**Progressions:**
- **Stopped twice in a row ends the set.**
- A try after three rucks counts double.
- **Coach's Bag:** Jeff holds up a number at every breakdown. A point only counts if the named player calls the number from the previous breakdown.

**Adaptations:**
- Calls being argued: drop a cone at each ruck.
- Attack over every time: a touch on the gain line counts as Stopped.
- Flagging: sets of four.

## Activities

### Crazy Passing — mixed balls

**Groups:** two groups of nine — three lines of three.

**Coaching Points:** **hands up before the ball comes** — a target, fingers spread. **Watch it into the hands.** Pass in front of him, not at him.

**Setup:** three cones in a line, 5m apart, on each try line. Mixed balls in a pile.

**Description:**

1. Ball zig-zags down the lines; follow your pass to the back of the line you passed to.
2. Rugby ball first, until the pattern is clean.
3. **Coach swaps the ball** — football, small ball, a different-size rugby ball. Keep it running.
4. Second ball in — a different one.
5. Third ball for the last two minutes.

**Progressions:**
- Two different balls in at once.
- Cones in to 3m.
- Receivers on the move — jog onto it.

**Adaptations:**
- Pattern breaking down: back to one rugby ball.
- Too easy: smallest ball only.

### Presentation Touch

**Groups:** two games — 5 v 4 on each pitch.

**Coaching Points:** **turn as you go down** — back to the defence. **Present long** — both arms back towards your own team. Support calls early and picks eyes-up.

**Setup:** the two game pitches. Touch.

**Description:**

1. Touch. On a touch the carrier goes to ground and presents long.
2. Support picks and plays. Not contested.
3. Defence back onside before it comes up.
4. **Ball not away in two seconds: turnover.**
5. Dropped ball or forward pass: turnover.

**Progressions:**
- A nominated 9 clears every ball.
- One defender stands over the ball without competing.

**Adaptations:**
- Sloppy presentations: walk it for three phases.
- Attack scoring too easily: narrow to 20m.

### Pop-up Touch — call the pop

**Groups:** same games — 5 v 4.

**Coaching Points:** **support calls "Pop!"** — loud, from close. **Carrier waits for the call**, then pops. Eye contact before the pop.

**Setup:** same pitches. Touch.

**Description:**

1. Touch. Carrier goes to ground.
2. **Carrier waits on the floor until the arriving support calls "Pop!"**
3. Pop up to the caller; he plays on.
4. **Pop with no call: turnover.**
5. No call inside two seconds: turnover.

**Progressions:**
- Coach calls "Pop" or "Present" at each touch.
- Pop must go to the second arriver, not the first.

**Adaptations:**
- Support never there: allow a pass instead of a pop for one phase in three.
- Too easy: one-second limit.

### Contact warm-up (whole squad)

**Groups:** whole squad, in pairs matched by size.

**Coaching Points:** in the pummel — chest up, back flat, **head to the side**, hips low, arms working continuously. In Wrist Wrestle — stay low, feet moving, win it with body position.

**Setup:** pairs, **matched by size**. No equipment.

**Description:** the standard two minutes.

1. **Get low (~30s)** — on the spot, feet moving, drop into the tackle-ready position. Five or six reps.
2. **Pummelling (~45s)** — pairs chest to chest **on knees**, one arm inside and one outside, both swimming the outside arm inside on the call.
3. **Wrist Wrestle (~45s)** — standing, same-wrist grips locked, free hand behind the back, slap with the held hand. A point per clean touch, then reset.

Pummelling: no lifting, no takedowns, no twisting a neck. Wrist Wrestle: **shoulder or upper arm only**, stops the moment a grip goes. **Anyone who would rather not play the slapping game pummels again instead — offer it up front.**

### Tug and Jackle

**Groups:** pairs matched by size — two groups, a coach each.

**Coaching Points:** **winner: turn your back on him and present long** — ball placed, body between it and the jackler, keep moving. **Loser: up, feet behind the ball, hips low, hands on it.** Fast — both of you.

**Setup:** pairs spread across halfway, a few metres apart. One ball per pair.

**Description:**

1. Pair lie on their fronts, **head to head**, both holding one ball.
2. Coach shouts **"Go!"** — tug for the ball. Ball only.
3. **Winner** turns away and presents on the floor to an imaginary team mate behind him.
4. **Loser** jumps up and jackles — on his feet, from his own side.
5. **Coach counts two.** Jackler's hands on the ball before two: his point. Otherwise the presenter's.
6. Swap ends, go again.

**Progressions:**
- A third player arrives on the presenter's side, calls "Pop!" and takes it.
- Tug starts on the coach's whistle from a sit-up, not lying down.
- Rotate partners every three reps.

**Adaptations:**
- **Pair by size.** Swap anyone badly mismatched.
- Jackler diving in off his feet: walk the jackle, then go again.
- Ground hard: jackle hands only, no weight on the presenter.

### Ruckly-Touch

**Groups:** same two games — 5 v 4.

**Coaching Points:** **present long, then support in fast** — two in, ball back. **Jackler: on your feet, through the gate.** Two seconds.

**Setup:** same pitches. Touch, contested rucks.

**Description:**

1. Touch. Carrier goes to ground and presents long.
2. **The first defender there may jackle** — on his feet, from his own side.
3. Attack support clears him — bound, low, drive him off the ball. No flying in.
4. Ball out, play on. **Ruck over two seconds: turnover.**
5. Jackler wins it clean: turnover.

**Progressions:**
- **1m / 1s rule:** touched carrier with a support player within 1m inside 1s may offload; otherwise ground and present.
- Two defenders may contest.

**Adaptations:**
- Defence winning every ruck: jackle only on an isolated carrier.
- Attack never contested: the toucher must jackle.

### Full contact game

**Groups:** same two games — 5 v 4. Swap a player across at each try.

**Coaching Points:** **two to the ruck, fast.** Present long. Support **talks** before the contact.

**Setup:** same pitches. Full contact to U14 laws.

**Description:**

1. Full contact. Tackle, ruck, play on.
2. Coach counts every ruck — **over two seconds: turnover.**
3. **1m / 1s** carries on if it was introduced in Ruckly-Touch.

**Progressions:**
- A try after three rucks counts double.
- Merge into one 9 v 9 game for the last ten minutes.

**Adaptations:**
- Ground hard or a mismatch: back to Ruckly-Touch.
- Rucks piling up: maximum two from each side.

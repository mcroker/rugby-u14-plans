## Activities

### Crazy Passing

**Groups:** three groups of eight — three lines in each.

**Coaching Points:** **hands up before the ball comes** — a target, fingers spread. **Catch it early, in front of the chest.** Pass in front of him, not at him.

**Setup:** three sets of three cones in a line, 5m apart, on one try line.

**Description:**

1. Ball zig-zags down the lines; follow your pass to the back of the line you passed to.
2. **Receivers jog onto it.**
3. Second ball in once the pattern is clean.
4. Third ball for the last two minutes.

**Progressions:**
- Cones in to 2m — pod distance.
- Mixed balls, as on Sunday.

**Adaptations:**
- Pattern breaking down: back to one ball.
- Casual hands in two or three: a spare coach takes them off the side, in pairs.

### Presentation Touch → Pop-up Touch

**Groups:** two games — 6 v 6 or 7 v 6 on each pitch.

**Coaching Points:** **turn as you go down** — back to the defence. **Present long** — both arms back towards your own team. Once popping: **support calls "Pop!"**, carrier waits for the call, eye contact before the pop.

**Setup:** the two small pitches. Touch.

**Description:**

1. Touch. On a touch the carrier goes to ground and presents long.
2. Support picks and plays. Not contested.
3. **Ball not away in two seconds: turnover.**
4. Dropped ball or forward pass: turnover.
5. **After three or four minutes, switch to Pop-up Touch** (below).

**Progressions:**
- **Pop-up Touch:** carrier waits on the floor until the arriving support calls **"Pop!"**, then pops up to the caller. Pop with no call, or no call inside two seconds: turnover.
- Referee calls "Pop" or "Present" at each touch.
- One-second limit.

**Adaptations:**
- Sloppy presentations: walk it for three phases before moving on.
- Support never there for the pop: allow a pass instead for one phase in three.

### Besties Touch

**Groups:** same two games.

**Coaching Points:** **know who is with you** — scan, then talk. **Support: get close and call your name.** Scorer: shout the name as the ball goes down.

**Setup:** same pitches. Pop-up Touch rules carry on.

**Description:**

1. Pop-up Touch, as before.
2. **A try only counts if the scorer shouts the name of his nearest supporting player** as he scores.
3. Referee judges it. Wrong name, no name, or nobody near: no try, ball to the defence.

**Progressions:**
- The named supporter must be inside 5m.
- The name must be called before the line, not after.

**Adaptations:**
- Players not knowing names: Everyone Shouts round the circle first.
- Tries drying up: name any supporter, not the nearest.

### 1m / 1s Touch

**Groups:** same two games.

**Coaching Points:** **support: get within 1m and call.** **Carrier: support there in time — offload; not there — down and present long.** Decide fast.

**Setup:** same pitches. Touch.

**Description:**

1. Touch.
2. **Support within 1m of the carrier inside 1s of the touch: carrier may offload** and play on.
3. **Otherwise: down and present long**, support picks — as in Presentation Touch.
4. Offload with no support in range: turnover. Ball not away in two seconds: turnover.
5. **Besties rule stays on** for tries.

**Progressions:**
- A try after an offload counts double.
- Pop-up instead of present when support calls "Pop!".

**Adaptations:**
- Referees can't judge 1m: "close enough to touch the carrier".
- Offloads forced every time: back to present only for two minutes.
- Attack scoring too easily: narrow the pitch.
